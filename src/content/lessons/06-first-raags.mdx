A raag is more than a scale: it has favourite notes, characteristic phrases and a mood. Two raags open the door for most bansuri students.

## Yaman

Yaman uses all seven swaras, but with **tivra (sharp) Ma** — played with every hole open. It often begins its ascent from the lower Ni instead of Sa.

## Bhupali

Bhupali is pentatonic: **Ma and Ni are left out**. With fewer notes, it's a wonderful raag for learning to make each note sing.

<Callout type="riyaz">
  With the tanpura on, play the pakad of each raag five times, slowly. Then improvise for two minutes using only the raag's notes, always returning to Sa.
</Callout>

**Alankars** (also called *paltas*) are short note patterns moved step by step up and down a scale. They are to Indian music what scales and arpeggios are to Western practice.

## Three classics

Groups of two:

<Sargam notes="S R R G G m m P P D D N N S'" bpm={90} />

Groups of three:

<Sargam notes="S R G R G m G m P m P D P D N D N S'" bpm={90} />

Skipping a note (*zig-zag*):

<Sargam notes="S G R m G P m D P N D S'" bpm={90} />

## Practice with the trainer

Pick a pattern and press play. Turn on **Echo mode** and the trainer leaves a gap after each group so you can play it back — a call and response with yourself.

<Callout type="tip">
  Speed comes from evenness, not effort. Only raise the tempo by 4–6 bpm once a pattern feels completely relaxed.
</Callout>

<Callout type="riyaz">
  Try the same patterns in **Bhupali** (five notes) and **Kafi** (komal Ga and Ni). Notice how the mood changes with just a few different swaras.
</Callout>

A **taal** is a repeating rhythmic cycle. Each beat is a **matra**; matras are grouped into sections called **vibhag**; one full turn of the cycle is an **āvartan**.

## The shape of Teentaal

Teentaal has 16 matras in four vibhags of four. Three vibhags are marked with a clap (**taali**) and one with a wave of the hand (**khali**) — that's where the name "three-clap" comes from.

- **X — sam**: matra 1, the strongest beat. Phrases tend to land here.
- **2** and **3**: claps on matras 5 and 13.
- **0 — khali**: matra 9, an "empty" wave. You can hear it too — the bass drum drops out (Dha becomes Ta).

<Callout type="riyaz" title="Keep taal with your hands">
  Switch the sound to **Claps only**. Clap on X, 2 and 3, wave on 0, and tap your fingers on the beats in between. When that feels easy, switch back to the tabla and count the matras aloud.
</Callout>

## A steady pulse first

If the full cycle feels like too much, start with a plain pulse. Set the metronome to 4 beats and count "1 2 3 4" — four bars of that is one Teentaal.
